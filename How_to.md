# How to make a peanut butter and jelly sandwhich

Peanut butter and jelly sandwiches are one of the most nostalgic foods in America. Everyone has had peanut butter and jelly sandwiches sometime in their childhood. Part of the reason they are such a big part of our childhoods is how simple they are, with its name being the entirety of the sandwich.

## Required Materials

- 2 Slices of bread
- 1 butter knife
- 1 jar of peanut butter
- 1 jar of preferred flavor of jelly

## Steps
- [ ] Unscrew the jar of peanut butter and the jar of jelly
- [ ] Set pieces of bread side by side 
- [ ] Use butter knife and spread peanut butter over one slice of bread 
- [ ] Make sure the peanut butter covers the entire surface of the bread including the sides
- [ ] Use butter knife and spread jelly over the other slice of bread
- [ ] Make sure the jelly covers the entire surface of the bread including the sides
![Placeholder](https://static.vecteezy.com/system/resources/previews/048/026/556/large_2x/peanut-butter-and-jelly-bread-cartoon-free-png.png) 
- [ ] Place the jelly-covered slice on top of the peanut butter slice
- [ ] Gently push down on sandwhich to evenly distribute filling
- [ ] Cut sandwhich into preferred shape and enjoy!
